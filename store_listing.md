## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
PetSalud keeps every pet's health history in one place — vet visits, vaccinations, medications, and more — stored right on your phone.

Whether you're caring for one pet or a whole household of them, PetSalud gives each one their own complete record, so nothing gets forgotten between vet visits.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK, PER PET
• Vet visits, hospitalizations, and procedures/surgeries
• Vaccinations, with automatic reminders for the next due date
• Illnesses and other custom health events
• Medications — dosing schedules, titration (step-down doses over time), and a real log of every dose given, with a reminder that automatically advances to the next dose
• Microchip records
• Notes and the people involved in a pet's care (e.g. which vet handled a visit)

STAY ON TOP OF WHAT'S DUE
Reminders link back to the record that created them — snooze, reschedule, or mark them done, and they stay in sync with the record itself. A dashboard shows what's due or overdue at a glance, and a full timeline lets you search and filter every record across every pet.

BUILT LOCAL-FIRST
PetSalud doesn't ask you to create an account, and it doesn't need one. Every record is stored directly on your device — there's no server, no sign-in, and no data collected, transmitted, or sold. Your pets' health history is yours alone.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose from seven color themes — a dark default, a light theme, and five soft pastel palettes — so the app looks the way you want it to.

Whether you're managing a single cat's vaccination schedule or coordinating medications across a multi-pet household, PetSalud is built to keep it all straight — privately, and entirely on your own device.
```
